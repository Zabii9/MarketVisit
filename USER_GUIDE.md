## 1. Overview

The **Daily Market Visit** platform enables real-time field data collection and analytics for market visits across distribution partners (such as CBL, Olpers Karachi, Olpers Lahore, Tapal, and others).

### Key Features:
- 📝 **Fast Field Entry**: Mobile-optimized form with camera capture and smart brand checkboxes.
- 📍 **GPS Verification**: Automatic location tagging for verified shop visits.
- 📊 **Real-time Analytics**: Interactive dashboard with maps, sales charts, and brand availability metrics.
- 💳 **Digital Payment Audit**: Track QR code adoption and payment gateway availability.
- 📱 **PWA Mobile App**: Add to smartphone Home Screen for fast app-like access.

## 2. Getting Started & Login

### Step 1: Open the Application
Navigate to your team's application web link in any modern browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Microsoft Edge, Brave).

### Step 2: Login to Your Account
1. Enter your assigned **Username** and **Password**.
2. Click **Sign In**.

> 💡 **User Roles**:
> - **Partner / Field User**: Assigned to specific distributor partner codes (e.g., Olpers KHI, CBL). You will only see data and options relevant to your assigned region.
> - **Administrator**: Has full access to all distributor partner codes, user management, and nationwide analytics.

## 3. Installing as a Mobile App (PWA)

You can install the application directly onto your Android or iPhone home screen without downloading from an app store.

### On Android (Chrome / Edge):
1. Open the app link in **Google Chrome**.
2. Tap the **Three Dots (⋮)** menu in the top-right corner.
3. Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install App"**.
4. Follow the prompt to confirm. An icon will appear on your phone home screen!

### On iOS (Safari):
1. Open the app link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share Button** (square with an up arrow at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.

## 4. Submitting a Daily Market Visit

Follow these steps to submit a market visit report:

### Step 1: Access the Visit Form
Click on **📝 Visit Form** from the top menu bar or mobile bottom navigation bar.

### Step 2: Fill Out Shop & Visit Information
- **Distributor Partner**: Select your distributor (e.g., `D70002202 - Olpers KHI`, `D0573 - CBL`).
- **Shop Name**: Enter the official name of the store.
- **Store Code**: Enter the internal store/distributor code (if applicable).
- **Area & Sub Area**: Enter the market locality/town and street/sector.
- **Booker Name**: Enter the name of the assigned order booker.

### Step 3: Capture Shop Picture & Selfie
- Tap the **Camera/Upload** button.
- Grant browser permission to use your phone camera if prompted.
- Take a clear photo showing the **Storefront along with yourself (Selfie)**.

### Step 4: Record Sales & Order Metrics
- **Shop Avg Monthly Sales (PKR)**: Enter estimated monthly turnover.
- **Last Order Booker Visit**: Select when the order booker last visited the shop.

### Step 5: Audit Digital Payment Gateways
- Select available payment options (e.g., *Cash, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer, Raast*).
- Check **QR Code Payment Available** if the merchant displays a QR code.
- Select estimated **QR Monthly Turnover**.

### Step 6: Audit Brand Availability
- **Top Brands Available**: Check off all active company brands visible in stock.
- **Competitor Brands Available**: Check off competitor brands displayed in the shop.

### Step 7: Remarks & GPS Location Verification
- Add any relevant market observations or supervisor notes in **Remarks**.
- Ensure **Location Services (GPS)** are enabled on your device.
- Click **🚀 Submit Market Visit**.
- The app will automatically fetch your precise coordinates and display a confirmation message upon success!

## 8. Troubleshooting & FAQs

### Q1: The form says "Location Permission Required" when submitting.
- **Fix**: Make sure your phone's GPS/Location is turned ON in phone settings. When your browser asks for location access, tap **Allow**.

### Q2: Camera is not opening on my phone.
- **Fix**: Check your browser site settings and ensure **Camera Access** is set to **Allow** for this website.

### Q3: My market visit submission failed or timed out.
- **Fix**: Check your internet connectivity. The form validates all required fields before sending. If offline, keep the tab open and click submit once internet connection is restored.
